**Turn idle compute across your app's device footprint into recurring revenue.**

Edge Providers connect devices to the ZeroGPU network and earn from the AI inference those devices serve. Add a ZeroGPU Edge SDK to an app you already ship, or install ZeroGPU Edge on the machines you manage. While a device is idle, it serves small, fast inference tasks locally. ZeroGPU handles routing, model delivery and verification, so there are no servers for you to run.

## How you earn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register">
    Sign up in the [Edge Operator Portal](https://edge.zerogpu.ai/auth/sign-up) and add your app or GPU fleet. Once it's approved, you get its SDK key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect devices">
    Add the SDK to your app, or install ZeroGPU Edge on your machines, with that key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Serve inference">
    Connected devices serve inference requests from ZeroGPU customers while they're idle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid">
    You earn a revenue share of every request your devices serve. Track devices, requests and earnings on the portal's **Analytics** page, and withdraw from **Withdrawals**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose your platform

### In your app

Add an SDK to an app your users already run. Each device running the app contributes while it's in use.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Android" icon="android" href="/docs/edge-providers/sdk/android">
    `ai.zerogpu:android-sdk` for Android apps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chrome / Web" icon="chrome" href="/docs/edge-providers/sdk/web">
    Chrome extensions (Manifest V3) and websites.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Telegram Mini Apps" icon="telegram" href="/docs/edge-providers/sdk/telegram">
    One script tag in your Mini App.
  </Card>
  <Card title="macOS apps" icon="apple" href="/docs/edge-providers/sdk/macos">
    `@zerogpu/worker` for Electron desktop apps.
  </Card>
</Columns>

### On your machines (GPU Fleet)

Install ZeroGPU Edge on Windows, macOS or Linux machines you manage, such as PCs, labs, servers or cloud VMs. It runs as a background service with no app or signed-in user needed.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Windows" icon="windows" href="/docs/edge-providers/fleet/windows">
    Windows 10 and 11, x64.
  </Card>
  <Card title="macOS" icon="apple" href="/docs/edge-providers/fleet/macos">
    macOS 12+, Apple Silicon and Intel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Linux" icon="linux" href="/docs/edge-providers/fleet/linux">
    Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora, RHEL, Rocky. x86_64 and ARM64.
  </Card>
</Columns>
