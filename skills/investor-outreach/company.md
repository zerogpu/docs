Positioning note for the drafter:
Dappier is the payment and access-control layer between publisher content and the AI agents/advertisers that want to use it. Rather than fighting AI scraping outright, it turns content into a licensable, monetizable asset - RAG feeds, on-site AI chat, sponsored conversational ads, and now an MCP-based storefront so AI agents can transact directly. The wedge is the "agent economy" distribution layer for publishers who don't want to build monetization infra themselves, not a foundation model or a single-point ad product.
