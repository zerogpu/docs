## How earnings work

You earn a revenue share of every inference request your devices serve. Earnings follow served requests, not connected time: a device that's online but hasn't been sent work yet earns nothing until it serves its first request.

How much work a device gets depends on network demand and on the device itself: how much memory and compute it has, how reliably it stays connected, and how long it has been part of the network. New devices get less work at first.

## Track requests and earnings

Open **Analytics** in the [Edge Operator Portal](https://edge.zerogpu.ai). Filter by app and date range.

| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Edge Requests** | Requests your devices served |
| **Earnings** | What those requests earned |
| **Unique Devices** | Devices seen over time |
| **Device Fleet** | A map of devices seen in the last 24 hours |

On **My Apps**, an app or fleet that's serving traffic shows as **Active/Earning**.

<Tip>
  Analytics can lag real time by a few minutes. If devices are connected but earnings stay at zero for more than a day, work through [Verify devices](/edge/verify), then contact [hello@zerogpu.ai](mailto:hello@zerogpu.ai) with your app name and platform.
</Tip>

## Break earnings down

Tag the work each device serves with up to three reporting labels (`cust_param1`–`cust_param3`), for example by site, lab or customer segment. Use values only (letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `-`), never personal data.

- **Agent:** `zerogpu-edge configure --cust-param1 …`, or the `CUST_PARAMn` MSI properties. See [agent configuration](/edge/agent#configuration).
- **SDKs:** the [Android](/edge/sdk/android) and [Chrome / Web](/edge/sdk/web) guides show where to pass them.

To break earnings down by reporting label, or pull them into your own dashboards and payout systems, use the [Reporting API](/edge/reporting-api).

## Withdraw

Withdraw your balance from **Withdrawals** in the portal.
